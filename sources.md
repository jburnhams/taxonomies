# Sources

Every source read or cited for this repo, grouped by field. Each has a folder in [`sources/`](./sources) with the full citation, access, what was read and notes. **Read** means read online on the date shown; **part** means read in part; **not read** means the notes come from prior knowledge of a well-known work and are marked for checking. Paywalled and unread items Jonathan could fetch are listed in [`to-fetch.md`](./to-fetch.md).
