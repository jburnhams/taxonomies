# To fetch

Papers and books that were paywalled, out of print, or blocked from this cloud container, in the order they would most improve the research. Jonathan offered to fetch these. Drop each file into its folder under [`sources/`](./sources) and the next pass will read it and replace the "from prior knowledge" notes.

Separately, the container's network policy refused every publisher and preprint host (arxiv.org, w3.org, niso.org, springer, PMC and others), so even the **free** items could not be downloaded and stored; they were read through a web reader instead. Those are listed last.
