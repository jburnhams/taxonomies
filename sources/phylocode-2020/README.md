## What is distinctive

A rank-free naming system: names are defined by what they pick out, not by their rank.

## Notes

- Drops mandatory ranks (genus, family...). A name is tied to a clade by an explicit **phylogenetic definition** ("the smallest clade containing A and B"), with specifiers as anchors.
- Shows that ranks are a convention that causes churn (move a group and its rank-ending name changes); defining a name by its members and anchors is more stable.
- For us: do not bake the level into a term's name (no "sub-", "meta-" prefixes as structure); define terms by anchors.
