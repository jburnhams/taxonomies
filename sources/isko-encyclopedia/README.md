## Notes

- Gives PMEST and Vickery's 13 CRG categories (see those folders).
- Hjørland's critique: facet analysis is rationalist and a priori; its "main weaknesses are (1) its lack of empirical basis and (2) its speculative ordering of knowledge"; logical division is used "at the expense of other methods of classification".
- Hjørland's own position (pragmatism): a classification is good relative to a purpose and a community, not true or false in itself. This is the position our design takes.
