## What is distinctive

The standard way to measure whether two people classify things the same way, beyond chance.

## Notes

- Cohen's kappa = (observed agreement - chance agreement) / (1 - chance agreement).
- Landis and Koch's benchmarks (often criticised, still used): 0.41-0.60 moderate, 0.61-0.80 substantial, above 0.80 almost perfect.
- For us: the objective test that a facet's values and definitions are clear enough. Two raters (Jonathan and a model, or two models with the glossary only) classify the same sample; low kappa on a facet means rewrite its definitions.
