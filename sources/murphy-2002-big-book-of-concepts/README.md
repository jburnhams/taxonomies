## What is distinctive

The fair-minded survey of the three theories of concepts after the classical view: prototype, exemplar and knowledge (theory) views.

## Notes

- **Prototype view**: a category is a summary of typical features, weighted.
- **Exemplar view**: a category is the remembered examples; new cases are compared to them.
- **Knowledge view**: categories are embedded in what we know about causes and purposes; that is why "things to take from a burning house" is a coherent category.
- Murphy's verdict: each explains some data; none explains all. For us that argues for storing a definition **and** examples **and** the purpose of a category.
