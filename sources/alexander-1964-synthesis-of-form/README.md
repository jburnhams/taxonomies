## What is distinctive

Alexander's mathematical start: decompose a design problem by clustering the requirements that interact.

## Notes

- Design is achieving **fit** between form and context; list the possible **misfits**, link the ones that interact, and decompose the graph into nearly independent clusters. Each cluster gets a "diagram", the ancestor of a pattern.
- In effect a classification method (numerical clustering of requirements), and the root of the idea that you name the recurring clusters.
- The 1971 preface says the diagrams were the lasting part, not the method; that is the turn to patterns. *(From prior knowledge; check.)*
