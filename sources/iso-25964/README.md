## What is distinctive

The international thesaurus standard, with a data model and rules for mapping between vocabularies.

## Notes

- Part 1: thesaurus construction (like Z39.19) plus a formal data model, which SKOS-XL can carry.
- Part 2: mapping between vocabularies (equivalence, hierarchical and associative mappings), which is the problem of linking our per-domain vocabularies.
