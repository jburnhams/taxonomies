## What is distinctive

The practitioner's book on taxonomies inside organisations; its catalogue of taxonomy forms is the useful part.

## Notes

- Forms of taxonomy, from simplest: **lists**, **trees**, **hierarchies**, **polyhierarchies**, **matrices**, **facets**, **system maps**. Each suits a different job (find, sort, explain, compare).
- Taxonomies as **boundary objects** between groups (after Star), and the work of getting a group to agree on one.
- Emphasis on purpose first and on governance after launch.
