## What is distinctive

Folk taxonomies across many cultures share the same shape, which says something about what people can handle.

## Notes

- Folk biological classifications have a few ranks (unique beginner, life form, intermediate, **generic**, specific, varietal) and are shallow, rarely more than five or six levels.
- The **generic** rank (oak, robin) is the most salient: named first, learned first, most numerous (typically a few hundred taxa), and named with simple words. Lower ranks get compound names (white oak).
- For us: keep taxonomies shallow, make the most-used level the one with short simple names, and let finer levels use compound names built from the generic.
