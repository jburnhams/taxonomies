## What is distinctive

Separates the two ways of making a classification: reasoning from concepts (typology) and grouping observed cases (taxonomy).

## Notes

- **Typology**: conceptual, built deductively from dimensions you choose (often a grid of ideal types).
- **Taxonomy** (in his sense): empirical, built inductively by clustering real cases.
- Most good schemes alternate the two. Nickerson et al. (2013) build their method on exactly that alternation (conceptual-to-empirical and empirical-to-conceptual iterations).
