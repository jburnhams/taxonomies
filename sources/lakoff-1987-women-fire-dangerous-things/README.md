## What is distinctive

The long argument against the classical view of categories (necessary and sufficient conditions), and what replaces it.

## Notes

- The title comes from the Dyirbal noun class *balan*, which groups women, fire and dangerous things, to show that categories hang together by chains of association, not shared features.
- **Radial categories**: a central case with extensions linked to it by different principles (mother: birth mother, adoptive mother, stepmother...). No single definition covers all members.
- **Idealised cognitive models (ICMs)**: categories are understood against background models. "Bachelor" fits a model of marriage that the Pope does not fit, which is why the Pope is a bad example of a bachelor even though he meets the definition.
- For us: a written definition is not enough on its own; record the central example and the known edge cases, and say which background assumption a term depends on.
