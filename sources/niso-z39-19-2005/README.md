## What is distinctive

The US standard for lists, synonym rings, taxonomies and thesauri: how to choose terms and relate them.

## Notes

- Four kinds of controlled vocabulary in increasing structure: **list**, **synonym ring**, **taxonomy**, **thesaurus**.
- **Warrant**: choose terms by literary warrant (the words in the documents), user warrant (the words users use) and organisational warrant (the words the organisation needs).
- **Equivalence** (USE / UF, "use for"), **hierarchy** (BT / NT: generic, whole-part, instance), **association** (RT).
- **Scope notes** say what a term includes and excludes.
- Term-form rules: nouns or noun phrases; plural for count nouns and singular for mass nouns and processes (an English-language convention that differs from ontology practice, which uses singular throughout); avoid abbreviations unless better known than the full form.
