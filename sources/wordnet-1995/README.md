## What is distinctive

The largest hand-built map of English word senses; useful for checking whether a candidate name is ambiguous.

## Notes

- Organises words into **synsets** (sets of synonyms for one sense) linked by hypernymy (is-a), meronymy (part-of) and more.
- Useful as a tool: look up a candidate name; many senses means a risk of homonymy in plain English.
