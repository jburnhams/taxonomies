## What is distinctive

Nickerson's method extended with goals, evaluation and maintenance; the best current method paper.

## Notes

- Reviewed 164 IS taxonomy articles: 160 built a taxonomy, but only **56 (34%) evaluated it**; 123 of 160 (77%) used Nickerson's method.
- An 18-step **extended taxonomy design process** in six phases: identify problem; define objectives (including evaluation goals); design and development (Nickerson's loop, plus a new step for "taxonomy operations"); demonstration (objective ending conditions); evaluation; communication.
- Do "at least one empirical-to-conceptual iteration and at least one conceptual-to-empirical iteration".
- Evaluation in two parts: **ex ante** ("Is it a taxonomy?" and "Is it an applicable taxonomy?", by the designers) and **ex post** ("Is it a useful taxonomy?", with users). Each needs "a triad of evaluation methods, evaluation criteria, and target values/thresholds".
- Evaluation goals: describing, identifying, classifying, analysing, clustering.
- **Taxonomy operations** for maintenance: add; update (rename, swap, split, merge, promote, demote); delete. Our change log uses this list.
- 26 design recommendations (TDRs) across the steps.
