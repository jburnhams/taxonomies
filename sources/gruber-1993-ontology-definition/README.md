The standard definition of an ontology in computing.

## Notes

- An ontology is "an explicit specification of a conceptualization". Studer et al. (1998) added "formal" and "shared".
- The difference from a taxonomy: an ontology adds typed relations, properties and axioms that a machine can reason over. Most of our need is met by a vocabulary plus a few relations; a full ontology is optional.
