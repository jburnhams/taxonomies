## What is distinctive

The theory behind *A Pattern Language*: patterns as a shared language that lets ordinary people generate good places.

## Notes

- Pattern languages are **generative**: like a natural language, a finite set of patterns can produce endless different designs.
- A town's language is shared by its people; when the language dies (handed to specialists), places get worse. The claim behind making the vocabulary shared, not owned by one expert.
- "The quality without a name": the goal is a quality that resists naming; the patterns are how you approach it. Critics find this vague.
