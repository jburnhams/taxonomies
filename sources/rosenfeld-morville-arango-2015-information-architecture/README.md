## What is distinctive

The information architecture textbook: organisation schemes, labelling systems and how to research them.

## Notes

- **Organisation schemes**: exact (alphabetical, chronological, geographical) vs ambiguous (by topic, task, audience, metaphor), and hybrids. Ambiguous schemes are more useful and harder to agree.
- **Labelling systems**: labels must be consistent in style, granularity, audience and syntax; draw them from users' language (search logs, interviews, card sorts).
- Introduced controlled vocabularies and thesauri to web practitioners.
