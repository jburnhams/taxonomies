## What is distinctive

A workshop method for discovering a domain's language fast, with sticky notes.

## Notes

- Everyone writes domain events (past tense: "Order placed") on a long wall in time order; conflicts in words show up as hotspots.
- A practical way to harvest a vocabulary from the people who use it, and to spot where one word means two things (a bounded-context boundary).
