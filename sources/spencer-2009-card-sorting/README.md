## What is distinctive

The handbook for the main user-research method for testing categories and names.

## Notes

- **Open sort**: people group cards and name the groups (finds their categories and words). **Closed sort**: people place cards into given groups (tests yours). **Hybrid**: both.
- Analysis by eye and by similarity matrix and dendrogram; the qualitative "why" matters more than the numbers.
- Follow with **tree testing** (can people find things in the proposed hierarchy?) before committing.
