## What is distinctive

Classify by shared origin, not overall likeness: the founding text of cladistics.

## Notes

- Groups should be **monophyletic** (an ancestor and all its descendants), recognised by shared derived characters.
- A grouping principle stated up front (common descent) beats ad hoc similarity, because it tells you what a group means and what evidence counts.
- For us: a taxonomy needs one stated principle of division per facet; Hennig is the strongest example of a field choosing one.
