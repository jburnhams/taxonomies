## What is distinctive

The opposite approach to Hennig: classify by overall similarity, measured, across many characters. The ancestor of cluster analysis.

## Notes

- Score many characters with equal weight, compute similarity, cluster. Groups come from data, not theory.
- Card sorting analysis (similarity matrices, dendrograms) and LLM clustering (TnT-LLM, Clio) are its descendants.
- Lesson from the fight between phenetics and cladistics: clusters show what looks alike; they do not tell you what the groups *mean*. Use clustering to find candidates, then name and define by purpose.
