## What is distinctive

"There are only two hard things in Computer Science: cache invalidation and naming things."

## Notes

- Attributed to Phil Karlton (Netscape); Fowler collects the variants (the "off-by-one errors" addition is Leon Bambrick's).
- Jonathan's starting point. The research says naming is hard for measurable reasons (Furnas, Feitelson) and that the fix is procedural (agree, record aliases, define, anchor), not finding the perfect word.
