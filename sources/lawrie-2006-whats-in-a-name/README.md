## What is distinctive

Experiment on identifier forms: full words vs abbreviations vs single letters.

## Notes

- Full-word identifiers gave the best comprehension; abbreviations were often nearly as good; single letters were worst.
- With Hofmeister et al. 2017, it supports the naming rule "spell words out; avoid abbreviations".
