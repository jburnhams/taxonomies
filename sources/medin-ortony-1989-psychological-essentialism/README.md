## What is distinctive

People behave as if categories have hidden essences, even when they cannot say what they are.

## Notes

- People believe a thing's kind is fixed by something deeper than its looks (a painted raccoon is still a raccoon).
- This is why renaming a category feels like changing what things *are*, and why naming fights get heated. It also explains the pull toward one "true" taxonomy.
