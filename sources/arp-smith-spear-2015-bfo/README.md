## What is distinctive

The realist school of ontology building, with a top-level split every domain can sit under.

## Notes

- BFO's top split: **continuants** (things that persist: objects, qualities, roles) vs **occurrents** (things that happen: processes, events).
- Rules of thumb that carry over: define terms Aristotle-style (genus plus differentia), single inheritance in the asserted hierarchy, univocal terms, reuse before you invent.
- For us: the continuant/occurrent split is a good first facet check (is this term a thing, a property, a role, or a happening?).
